Antlerwood is a fusion of two difficult, rare materials: solid wood and natural antler, joined by hand with traditional tools.

About us

Antlerwood was born from a love of natural materials and pieces that can never be made the same way twice. We combine solid wood with antler to create furniture and unique objects with character.

Every project starts with an idea, but nature often suggests the final form. The pattern of the grain, the shape of the wood or the line of the antler make each piece one of a kind.

How we work

Everything in-house, no shortcuts

We work with traditional tools, chisels and Japanese saws, giving each piece a hand-made character a machine can't reproduce. Over time we add CNC for larger runs while keeping the craft detail.
